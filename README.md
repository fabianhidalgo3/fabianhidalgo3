#### Featured projects

**⚡ EV yard power control.** Real-time power allocation for electric-vehicle charging yards. An OCPP 1.6J central
system keeps the yard under its contracted limit and shares the available power among the vehicles. It comes with a
live dashboard, energy-savings indicators, predictive maintenance and an AI operations assistant. Thesis project.
